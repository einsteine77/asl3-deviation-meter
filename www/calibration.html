DEVIATION— kHz
  1. RX: key a radio or service monitor into the receiver with a steady 1 kHz tone at a known deviation (3.0 kHz is typical). Wait until the side reads RX, then enter the deviation and press Calibrate.
  2. TX: send a steady tone through the network (a parrot or another node), read the transmitter's deviation on a service monitor, and enter it while the tone is still playing.
  3. Calibrate uses the median peak of the last 2 seconds on that side. If you already know the kHz-at-full-scale figure, enter it directly.
